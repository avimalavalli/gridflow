# Phase 8D — Professional product finish

Phase 8D refines the complete pre-security product without expanding the commercial scope or weakening an approval gate. It replaces phase-by-phase visual growth with one restrained interface, puts the working flow ahead of internal tool names and removes development language from customer-facing screens.

## Product-wide refinement

- A single visual layer now governs colour, spacing, typography, radii, shadows, controls, tables, metrics, empty states, dialogs, onboarding, authenticated workspaces and public pages.
- Decorative gradients, oversized effects, inconsistent card treatments and hover movement are removed or neutralised. Status meaning remains visible through restrained state colour.
- Body, table, form, queue and operational text is larger and more legible. Compact labels are no longer used as a substitute for hierarchy.
- User-facing enum codes are rendered as normal language, with product-specific capitalisation such as LinkedIn and Gmail.
- Page introductions are shorter and functional. Build phases no longer appear in the product interface.

## Simpler working flow

Navigation now follows the real commercial lifecycle:

1. Command Centre
2. Discovery Briefs
3. Companies
4. Contacts
5. Outreach
6. Opportunities
7. Delivery
8. Renewals

Commercial tools, planning records, learning and management are separate collapsible groups. The current group opens automatically, the core workflow remains immediately available and role/platform restrictions remain unchanged.

The command search uses plain destinations, records and tool names. Internal agent brands remain available where they help operators understand provenance, while primary navigation describes the job the user is trying to complete.

## Workflow and copy controls

- Automation is presented as controls and approvals, not an autonomous “autopilot”.
- Pulse, Sentinel, Nova, Orbit, Forge and Seal keep their underlying traceability while their main workspaces use the clearer labels Follow-ups, Reply Inbox, Response Strategy, Meeting Intelligence, Proposals and Contracts.
- Provider spend and research usage are described accurately without turning every screen into an AI dashboard.
- Onboarding uses five plain sections, automatically saves progress and separates profile, commercial context, target criteria, outreach controls and final provider review.
- Data failures, empty states, status badges and form choices use consistent, actionable language.
- Public product language states what the system does and where human control remains, without fabricated proof or inflated claims.

## Commercial constraints preserved

- Core is individually quoted for each driver. No fixed onboarding amount is stored or displayed.
- AM Motorsports Ltd receives verified GBP payments through Wise Business; no public checkout, card collection or recurring billing was introduced.
- Core remains permanent with 500 starter research credits, one named driver and at most two trusted devices.
- Ultra remains an optional configured 30-day period with included credits, no automatic renewal and extension from the existing expiry when renewed early.
- LinkedIn actions remain user-performed. External messages, relationships, meetings, opportunity changes, prices, legal terms and financial records retain their existing human-control boundaries.

## Release boundary

Phase 8D is the last software refinement before Phase 9. Its merged commit invalidates any earlier exact-commit product freeze by design. The real Phase 8A provider evidence, Phase 8B.2 economics window and Phase 8C internal desktop/mobile journeys must be completed or refreshed against the final pre-security commit before production approval.

Phase 9 security hardening is intentionally not part of this change.
